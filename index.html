About The Earnings Analyst

The Earnings Analyst (TEA) is a peer-reviewed academic journal that serves as the primary publication for professionals engaged in the economic analysis of personal injury, wrongful death, and disability cases. Founded as a collaborative effort between leading professional organizations in rehabilitation economics and pecuniary damages.

Journal Focus Areas:

  • Methodologies for calculating economic losses
  • Rehabilitation economics and life care planning
  • Labor market analysis and earnings capacity
  • Present value and discount rate applications
  • Statistical and econometric approaches
  • Legal and regulatory developments
  • Case law analysis and expert testimony standards
  • International perspectives on damages calculation

Publication Standards:

  • Rigorous peer-review process
  • Original research and methodology
  • Practical applications for practitioners
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration

Submissions Guidelines

Call for Papers

The Earnings Analyst welcomes original research contributions that advance the theory and practice of economic analysis in legal contexts. We seek manuscripts that demonstrate methodological rigor, practical relevance, and scholarly excellence.

Manuscript Categories:

  • Original Research Articles: Empirical studies, methodological innovations, theoretical contributions
  • Case Studies: Detailed analysis of complex cases with methodological insights
  • Review Articles: Comprehensive reviews of current practices and literature
  • Short Communications: Brief reports on preliminary findings or methodological notes
  • Letters to the Editor: Comments on published articles or current issues in the field

Submission Requirements:

  • Manuscripts must be original and not under review elsewhere
  • Word limit: 8,000 words for full articles, 3,000 for short communications
  • APA format for citations and references
  • Anonymous review copy (no author identifying information)
  • IRB approval documentation if applicable

Review Process

1. Initial Review

Editorial team conducts preliminary assessment for scope and quality

2. Peer Review

Double-blind review by expert reviewers in the field

3. Editorial Decision

Accept, revise, or reject decision with detailed feedback

4. Publication

Copy editing, proofing, and publication in next available issue

Editorial Mission

The editorial board is committed to maintaining the highest standards of scholarly excellence while ensuring the practical relevance of published research. We seek to bridge the gap between academic rigor and professional application, fostering innovation in methodology while preserving the fundamental principles of economic analysis.

Review Standards

  • Methodological soundness and statistical rigor
  • Practical relevance to professional practice
  • Clarity of presentation and reproducibility
  • Contribution to existing knowledge base
  • Ethical considerations and professional standards
