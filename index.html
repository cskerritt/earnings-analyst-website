About The Earnings Analyst

The Earnings Analyst is a premier publication dedicated to providing accurate, comprehensive economic analysis for legal professionals. Published as a joint venture between the Center for Professional Development in Economics (CPDE) and the Association of Regional Economic Analysts (AREA).

What We Offer:

  • Monthly economic analysis reports
  • Wage loss calculation methodologies
  • Labor market trend analysis
  • Regional economic data and insights
  • Expert testimony support materials
  • Case study reviews and best practices
